## Summary

PASS. The plan extends the right objects: the existing `control_server` handlers (not new roster/say/wait handlers), `authenticate_and_hello` unchanged with a branch after it, the body's `handshake::authenticate`, and `hello_exchange` with a role parameter instead of a second copy of the pinning logic. Every `say` still reaches the body through `send_prompt`. I checked the branch point in the code: `authenticate_and_hello` (circuit.rs:178-225) returns before `conn_connected`, supersede, `registry.insert` and `roster.set_*` (circuit.rs:261-299). The dependency direction also holds: holler-hub does not depend on holler-body, and the glue is in holler-cli. The findings below are `warn` only. Two of them (W-1 docs.rs line budget, W-3 x25519 `ensure` side effect) should be settled by F/T before or during RED.
