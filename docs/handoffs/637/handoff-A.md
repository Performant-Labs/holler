## Summary

BLOCK, with 2 blocks and 9 warns.

Revision 2 answers all six revision-1 blocks. I re-checked each one against the code:
- `main()` is at 99 of 100 lines.
- The hub's forwarding arm and handles reach the stubs.
- `holler-pane` → `holler-proto` adds no cycle.
- ADR 0003 moves with the surface.
- The `--pane` shapes parse on clap 4.6.6 with a variadic tail. I confirmed this in a scratch crate; it works only without `trailing_var_arg`.

The two new blocks come from revision 2's own freezes, checked against the sibling issues' fixed text:
- `pane/args.rs` and `profile/args.rs` are frozen, but they declare none of the positionals or verb-specific flags that nine verb stories need. Those stories' blast radii hold only their own verb files.
- The `PaneError` list is closed and incomplete. It misses two codes the issues name, and it has no variant for the timeouts, missing records, corrupt stores and unreachable services that the port implementers must return.

Both fixes are brief edits, not a redesign.
